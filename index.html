[ GOOD QUESTIONS. STRAIGHT ANSWERS. ]

Frequently asked questions.

What makes this gym different?+

Real strength training means appropriate loads, lower rep ranges, rest between efforts, and progressive overload over time. Our gym uses structured intervals and coaching instead of random workouts.

Why no barbells?+

Our training emphasizes free-moving equipment such as kettlebells, dumbbells, and dead balls. Coaches can scale movements and help members move with control and confidence.

I've tried other gyms and never seen real results. Will this be different?+

The program is designed around repeatable work, coaching, and progression. Consistency matters: the goal is to build strength over time, not simply to finish a hard workout.

I've never lifted weights before. Is that a problem?+

No. Movements can be taught from the ground up and loads can be adapted to each member. Let the coaching team know you're new so they can guide you through your first sessions.

I don't have time to work out four times per week.+

A session is 50 minutes. Four sessions add up to 3 hours and 20 minutes per week. Start with a schedule you can sustain and ask your studio about an appropriate starting plan.
